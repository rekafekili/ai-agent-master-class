## Predicted Interview Questions

### Technical Questions

1. **Golang Fundamentals:**  
   - Explain Go concurrency primitives (goroutines, channels) and how you have or would use them to build scalable, concurrent services.  
   - How do you manage memory and optimize performance in Go applications?

2. **System Design & Distributed Systems:**  
   - Design a scalable system capable of processing 22 million betting transactions monthly.  
   - How would you architect event-driven backend workflows using Kafka?  
   - Discuss strategies for handling data consistency and fault tolerance in distributed systems.

3. **Cloud & Automation:**  
   - Explain your experience with AWS services like CloudFormation, MSK, S3, and Lambda in building cloud-native apps.  
   - How do you implement CI/CD pipelines to automate deployment for reliability and speed?

4. **Observability & Monitoring:**  
   - What tools or approaches would you use to build observability into a high-transaction backend system?  
   - How can observability tooling be improved to prevent outages or improve performance?

5. **Programming & Testing:**  
   - Describe your approach to clean code, test automation, and continuous deployment in backend projects.  
   - Provide an example of refactoring code to increase maintainability and scalability.

6. **Functional Programming & Erlang (Bonus):**  
   - What interests you about functional programming, and how have you explored Erlang or other FP languages?  
   - What advantages might Erlang provide in distributed real-time systems?

- Tell me about a time you worked with a distributed team — how did you coordinate and ensure productive collaboration?  
- Describe a challenging project with tight deadlines — how did you balance speed and quality?  
- How do you approach learning new technologies, such as Golang and Erlang?  
- Give an example where you drove an initiative or task from start to finish in your team.

## Strategic Advice

### Tone & Demeanor

- Maintain a **confident and curious** tone that reflects eagerness to learn and adapt to new technologies like Golang and Erlang.  
- Show humility around gaps but highlight your rapid learning and relevant experience.  
- Balance technical depth with clarity, demonstrating a systems-thinking approach.  
- Emphasize collaboration and communication skills for working with distributed, multidisciplinary teams.

### Focus Areas

- Highlight your **cloud-native experience**, especially AWS infrastructure and automation skills.  
- Bring examples of **scaling backend systems** and improving performance or reliability.  
- Present your understanding or exposure to **distributed systems and event streaming**.  
- Stress your commitment to **clean code, testing, and continuous improvement** practices.  
- Discuss your **user-centric mindset and data-driven development** approach.  
- Show enthusiasm for **engineering culture growth and leadership initiatives**, even at a junior seniority level.

### Red Flags to Avoid

- Avoid over-reliance on previously used technologies without demonstrating willingness and plans to learn Golang and Erlang.  
- Don’t underestimate the importance of distributed system concepts or underprepare for system design discussions.  
- Steer clear of vague answers about collaboration or handling geographically distributed teams — be specific and example-driven.  
- Avoid underplaying the significance of observability and automation in a high-scale environment.  
- Don’t neglect behavioral questions — use STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) format to clearly communicate your experiences.
