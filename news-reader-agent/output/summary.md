### 📖 Comprehensive Summary  
The US and Israel launched a significant military offensive against Iran, dubbed Operation Epic Fury, aimed at eradicating Iran’s nuclear weapons capability and crippling its missile industry. This operation followed weeks of escalating threats by US President Donald Trump, who insisted on preventing Iran from acquiring nuclear weapons. On Saturday, coordinated air strikes involving around 200 Israeli fighter jets targeted multiple Iranian military installations, including the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) bases, missile and drone launch sites, and air defense facilities across Tehran, Karaj, Isfahan, Qom, and Kermanshah. The attacks culminated in the death of Iran’s Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, a pivotal figure in Iranian politics confirmed by Iran’s Supreme National Security Council.

The strikes reportedly killed more than 200 people and injured over 700, with at least 153 casualties from a school explosion in southern Iran. Intelligence sources revealed that around 40 Iranian officials, including top defense leaders such as Defence Council secretary Ali Shamkhani and IRGC commander Gen Mohammad Pakpour, were among the dead. Israel’s military declared the operation successfully paved the way to Tehran’s central districts, continuing with focused strikes on key command and control centers on Sunday.

In response, Iran condemned the operation as "wholly unprovoked, illegal, and illegitimate," launching retaliatory ballistic missile and drone assaults against US military bases and allied nations in the Gulf region. These attacks hit locations in Israel, Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar, the UAE, Jordan, Oman, and Saudi Arabia, causing significant infrastructural damage and civilian casualties, including a destroyed synagogue in Beit Shemesh with at least nine dead. The Pentagon reported three US service members killed and five injured, though many Iranian attacks were successfully intercepted. The EU naval mission in the Gulf warned about Iran’s attempts to block navigation through the Strait of Hormuz, a vital route for about 20% of the world’s oil shipments, and pledged increased maritime presence.

Amid the chaos, Alireza Arafi was appointed interim supreme leader, while the formal process to select Khamenei’s successor by the Assembly of Experts faces complications due to security concerns. The conflict has resulted in severe disruptions to global travel, with airlines suspending flights to and from the affected Middle Eastern region, and multiple countries closing airspace.

The international community remains on high alert, balancing between escalating military engagements and diplomatic efforts to prevent a broader regional war. The operation marks one of the most serious confrontations in recent Middle Eastern history, significantly increasing geopolitical instability and threatening global energy markets.
